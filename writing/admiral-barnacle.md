# Admiral Barnacle: dialogue

Admiral Barnacle runs Barnacle Salvage & Drilling. His sea mines are his "No Trespassing" signs, and his flagship is the Big Bad Barnacle.

## Wave 1, opening scene

When the first wave starts, he doesn't know the player exists. He is on the radio to his crews about the day's salvage. Then the player sinks one of his subs, and he slowly realises somebody is out there.

### 1. Orders to his crews (wave 1 begins)

He doesn't know the player is there yet.

- "Morning, you barnacle-brained bilge rats! Today we strip this reef bare. Coral, clams, shiny pebbles. If it glitters, it's mine!"
- "Crews one through six, start drilling. I want that reef in crates by lunchtime."
- "Scrap Crew, find me every wreck on this shelf. Old subs are full of lovely, lovely parts."
- "Blast that reef wide open, boys. There's treasure under those pretty little corals, and I want it."
- "Lay the mines, drill the reef, and if a fish complains, salvage the fish."

### 2. First enemy sub sunk

He's puzzled.

- "...Number Seven? Number Seven, why have you stopped drilling? Answer me!"
- "Hm? That sounded expensive."
- "Who turned off Sub Three? I didn't say anyone could sink. Sinking costs money!"
- "Crew Four, report! ...Crew Four? Why is Crew Four bubbles now?"

### 3. Second or third sub sunk

He spots the player.

- "Huh?! Who's that? Who is blowing up my subs?!"
- "What in the briny blazes is THAT? Is that... a yellow bathtub?"
- "Somebody is sinking my salvage fleet! And they're doing it in a submarine with a rubber duck on top!"
- "Hold everything. There's a SUB on my reef. A sub that isn't MINE."

### 4. End of wave 1

He declares war.

- "Nobody sinks a Barnacle sub and swims away! Send the whole fleet!"
- "Fine. FINE. You want trouble, little ducky? Barnacle Salvage delivers."
- "Write this down, boys. New job for tomorrow: salvage one yellow submarine."

## Taunts for later waves

- "Well, well. A bathtub with a periscope. Boys, scrap it for parts!"
- "These waters belong to Barnacle Salvage and Drilling. Trespassers get recycled!"
- "Didn't you see the signs? They're the round, spiky ones. They go boom."
- "Ahoy, little rubber ducky! Let's see how well you float with a few extra holes."
- "Every wreck down here was somebody's favourite sub once. Yours will fit right in."
- "I've drilled through bedrock tougher than you. Fire at will!"
- "A yellow submarine? How delightful. Yellow paint makes the best target practice."
- "Turn back now, and I'll only take half your sub. Stay, and I'll take the whole thing!"
